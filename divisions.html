// Institutional Review
Not Assigned.
Called In.

These analysts are not chosen directly. They enter when an investigation reaches a specific stage — not part of the department grid above, because they don't belong to one.

NIX
Cross-System Synthesis
// TRIGGERED WHEN
Multiple divisions complete findings on the same case. NIX compares them — agreement, conflict, convergence, open questions. Not another opinion. A check on whether the others actually agree.
ASH
Final Assessment
// TRIGGERED WHEN
An investigation is marked ready to close. ASH reviews the evidence, removes unsupported conclusions, names what remains, and closes the case. The last voice, not the first.
