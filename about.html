THE INSTITUTION

Not a horoscope.
A recovered archive.

Wild Sister was built because too many spaces offered certainty before investigation, labels before evidence, and performance before honesty.

Underneath Wild Sister runs the Symbol Intelligence Division — an investigative institution that studies recurring patterns across astrology, original text, numerology, gematria, symbolic traditions, history, and human behavior.

We are not revealing anything you were never supposed to find. We are recovering, organizing, and cross-referencing traditions that became separated across scripture, sky, number, language, and lived experience — placing them back into conversation so patterns can be investigated more clearly.

Eight analysts operate beneath every investigation and file. Six lead permanent research divisions. NIX is assigned when findings must be compared across systems. ASH enters only when a case is ready for final assessment and closure. The live community layer runs alongside the institution through research rooms, briefings, classes, and member discussion.

// INSTITUTIONAL MISSION

The Symbol Intelligence Division exists to investigate recurring patterns across symbolic systems and human experience through disciplined observation, cross-referenced evidence, and intellectual honesty.

// SID INVESTIGATION PROTOCOL
How the institution works.

Every SID investigation begins with reality before symbolism. The broader institutional protocol applies to archive files, division reports, member cases, and public research.

01
Observation
What happened? What can be directly described?
02
Evidence
What can actually be documented?
03
Verification
Can the evidence be independently confirmed?
04
Cross-Reference
Do separate sources identify the same structure?
05
Pattern Analysis
Does recurrence survive contradiction?
06
Integration
What conclusion is supported by the available evidence?
07
Final Assessment
What remains supported, unresolved, or ready for closure?

// OPERATING PRINCIPLES
The signal we run on.
Evidence before certainty.
Confidence follows support. Tone never replaces a documented basis.
Observation before interpretation.
The file separates what happened from what it may mean.
Context before conclusion.
No symbol, text, number, or chart placement is treated as context-free.
Cross-reference before confidence.
One source opens a question. Independent confirmation strengthens a finding.
Patterns before labels.
We do not reduce people to signs, placements, wounds, or archetypes.
Questions before answers.
A better question can be more useful than a premature conclusion.
SID RULE 001

Evidence before certainty.

SID RULE 002

Observation before interpretation.

SID RULE 003

No finding enters the archive without independent confirmation from at least two sources of evidence.
